Payment policy engine on Vela

An agent proposes payments. The enclave applies the owner's policy: what fits is paid at once through the trigger contract, the rest waits for the owner. Every action is one request to the enclave, 30 to 60 s on a devnet; a payment also runs the trigger cycle before it shows as settled.

What happens next

Once deployed: fund the treasury, allow payees, let the agent propose. A proposal inside the policy is paid at once; outside it, it waits here for your approval, with every reason. The chain sees one receipt per executed payment and nothing else.
